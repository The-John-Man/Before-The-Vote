# Short excerpts from City of Toronto guidance pages, copied 13 Nov 2026.
# From https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/tmmis/have-your-say.htm
Please register to speak by contacting the committee email listed on the relevant committee page. We request that you register by 4:30 p.m. the day before the meeting.
Communications not received in time will not be distributed to Members.
# From https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/council/council-committee-meetings/have-your-say/
Presenters are not heard at meetings of City Council, unless there is a legislated requirement to do so.
# From https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/council/council-committee-meetings/participate/
Public Presentations are limited to five minutes, unless the committee decides otherwise; keep track of your time while speaking.
The listing of your name in connection with an agenda item may be indexed by search engines like Google.
