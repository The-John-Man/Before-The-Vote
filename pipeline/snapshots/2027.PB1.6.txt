Toronto Preservation Board consideration on November 16, 2026

PB1.6 - 1337 Queen Street West - Demolition and New Construction on a Designated Heritage Property under Part V, Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act - Approval
Consideration Type: ACTION
Ward: 4 - Parkdale - High Park
Origin
(October 28, 2026) Report from the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, Urban Design, City Planning
Recommendations

The Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, Urban Design, City Planning recommends that:

1. City Council approve the demolition of the non-contributing building and the erection of a new 16-storey mixed-use building at 1337 Queen Street West, in the Parkdale Main Street Heritage Conservation District in accordance with subsections 42(1) 4 and 42(1) 2 of the Ontario Heritage Act, and subject to the following conditions:

a. that the replacement building for 1337 Queen Street West is constructed substantially in accordance with the plans and elevations prepared by LGA Architectural Partners dated September 25, 2026, on file with the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning.

Summary

This report recommends that City Council approve the demolition of an existing building on a non-contributing property located at 1337 Queen Street West within the Parkdale Main Street Heritage Conservation District (PHCD) and approve the erection of a replacement building in accordance with Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act in connection with the City-led Parkdale Hub project.

The proposed development is for the West Block of the Parkdale Hub project to erect a new 16-storey mixed-use tower with a 4-storey podium building that will include purpose-built affordable housing; and which will remain under ownership of the City.

A heritage permit for the project was previously approved on June 24 and 25, 2026, by City Council. The current proposal is largely the same as the previous approval, but a change has been made to the height of the podium of the new building. The street wall height of the new podium building has been increased by 1.2m which means that it no longer matches the height of the adjacent contributing building at 1347 Queen Street West. It also does not include the 5.0m step back required by the PHCD Plan for any parts of the new building that are taller than the adjacent contributing property. Consequently, a new Section 42 approval under the Ontario Heritage Act is required.

One of the objectives of the PHCD Plan is to conserve the consistent and continuous streetwall that characterises most of the PHCD but this section of south side of Queen Street West has neither a consistent nor a continuous street wall. Therefore, the proposed modest height increase for the podium of the new Parkdale Hub West Block development would not harm the character or appearance of the District or be contrary to the objectives of the PHCD Plan. As such staff have no concern with the lack of a step back of the top of the new podium building in this location.

Financial Impact

There are no financial implications resulting from the recommendations included in this report in the current budget year or in future years.

The Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer has reviewed this report and agrees with the information as presented in the Financial Impact Section.
