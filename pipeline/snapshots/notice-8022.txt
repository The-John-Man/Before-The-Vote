# Title: Notice of Intention to Remove a Holding Symbol (H) from Zoning By-law 992-2026 - 701 Fleet Street

NOTICE OF INTENTION TO REMOVE A HOLDING SYMBOL (H) FROM ZONING BY-LAW 992-2026
(Under the Planning Act)
The City has received the following application under Section 36 of the Planning Act :
Application Number: 26 257610 STE 10 OZ
Address: 701 Fleet Street
Ward: Ward 10 - Spadina-Fort York
Contact: George Pantazis, Senior Planner, Community Planning at 416-392-3566, by e-mail at George.Pantazis@toronto.ca , or by mail at 18th Floor East Tower, City Hall, 100 Queen Street West, Toronto ON M5H 2N1
DESCRIPTION
The application to amend Zoning By-law 992-2026 to remove the holding symbol (H) to permit the construction of a three-storey athletic training facility for the Toronto Tempo Basketball Club. The location of where the holding symbol (H) would be removed is noted on the map below.
Detailed information about the proposal, including background information and materials may be made available for review by contacting the Planner noted above or by visiting the City of Toronto’s Application Information Centre.
MAKE YOUR VIEWS KNOWN
You may send written comments by mail or email to the Community Planner noted under Contact. Your written comments must be submitted no later than November 27, 2026 to be considered by the Executive Director, Development Review or their designate, Director, Community Planning, Toronto and East York District. A decision by the Executive Director, Development Review or their designate as to whether to remove the holding symbol (H) will occur no earlier than December 15, 2026 .
Notice to people writing to the City : The City of Toronto Act, 2006 , the Planning Act , and the City of Toronto Municipal Code authorize the City of Toronto to collect any personal information in your communication to the City.
The City collects this information to enable it to make informed decisions on the relevant issue(s). If you are submitting letters, e-mails, or other communications to the City, you should be aware that your name and the fact that you communicated with the City will become part of the public record and will appear on the City's website.
