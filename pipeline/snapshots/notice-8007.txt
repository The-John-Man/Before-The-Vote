# Title: Notice of Public Meeting - Rental Housing Demolition - 411 and 415 Kennedy Road and 636-646 Danforth Road

NOTICE OF PUBLIC MEETING
To be held by the Scarborough Community Council
(Municipal Code Ch. 667 under City of Toronto Act, 2006 )
Rental Housing Demolition Application Number 20 211505 ESC 20 RH
Location of Application: 411 and 415 Kennedy Road and 636-646 Danforth Road
Applicant: SJ Masterplan Limited Partnership
Date: November 25, 2026
Time: 9:30 a.m., or as soon as possible thereafter
Place: Council Chamber, Scarborough Civic Centre, 150 Borough Drive and by Video Conference
PROPOSAL
The application proposes to demolish eight existing rental dwelling units at 636-646 Danforth Road and build a 23-storey mixed-use building with 276 residential units, including eight replacement rental dwelling units. Tenant relocation and assistance is proposed to lessen hardship for impacted tenants. The buildings at 636-646 Danforth Road are part of the larger site comprised of 411 and 415 Kennedy Road, 636-646 Danforth Road, 641-663 Danforth Road, and 3585 and 3595 St. Clair Avenue East. A related Zoning By-law Amendment application (20 211279 ECS 20 OZ) has been approved by the Ontario Land Tribunal for the lands at 646-646 Danforth Road and 411 and 415 Kennedy Road.
A Rental Housing Demolition and Conversion application (20 211505 ESC 20 RH) under Section 111 of the City of Toronto Act, 2006 (Chapter 667 of the Municipal Code) has been submitted to permit the demolition. Under the City of Toronto Act, 2006, the demolition of a rental property with six or more dwelling units is prohibited without obtaining a permit from the City. Decisions by City Council on the Rental Housing Demolition application are not subject to appeal to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT).
Detailed information regarding the demolition proposal, including background information and materials, may be obtained by contacting Meredith Gillespie, Planner, Strategic Initiatives, Policy and Analysis (Housing) at 416-394-4023, or by e-mail at Meredith.Gillespie2@toronto.ca .
PURPOSE OF PUBLIC MEETING
Scarborough Community Council will receive input and review the proposal and any other
material placed before it, in order to make recommendations on the application. These recommendations
will then be forwarded to Toronto City Council for its consideration.
You can follow the meeting at www.youtube.com/TorontoCityCouncilLive .
